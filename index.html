How this is counted

  • PnL is the sum of your settled BTC Up or Down 5m rounds, taken from your public XO portfolio (the same per-round numbers the app shows).
  • Day results use UTC days (00:00 to 24:00 UTC); today's keeps updating until the day ends.
  • While the competition dates are TBA, the race chart and "Find yourself" cover the last 7 days.
  • Private profiles aren't ranked. Make your profile public in XO settings to appear.
  • The Win rate tab needs at least 5 decided rounds that day.
  • This is a community page. XO Market's own results decide any prizes.
